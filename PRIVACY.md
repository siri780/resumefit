# ResumeFit Privacy Policy

ResumeFit is a personal tool used only by its author, Shirisha Gajendra.

- **Google Drive access:** ResumeFit uses the `drive.file` permission. It can only
  see and manage files that ResumeFit itself creates. It cannot read any other
  files in your Google Drive.
- **Gmail access:** ResumeFit uses the read-only `gmail.readonly` permission
  only to find job-alert emails from company career sites (Meta, Google,
  Microsoft, Apple) and read the job titles, locations and links in them.
  It cannot send, delete or change email, and it does not store or share
  email content; only the extracted job details are kept.
- **What is stored:** tailored resumes, job descriptions and an application
  tracker, saved to the author's own Google Drive and a private GitHub repository.
- **Sharing:** no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
- **AI:** resume bullets and job descriptions are sent to the OpenAI API to
  suggest edits. No other personal data is sent.
